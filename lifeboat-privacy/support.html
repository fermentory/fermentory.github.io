Try Lifeboat before you need it

In version 1.0.2, open Lifeboat in Chrome's toolbar and choose Test Lifeboat. The local test uses a disposable sample and the same save-and-restore code as a real page. It deletes the saved sample when the test finishes. It does not count as a real rescue.

Lifeboat didn't save my text

  • Under 25 characters isn't saved. That floor stops Lifeboat from hoarding every search box and one-word reply. Pro lets you change it, anywhere from 1 to 200 characters.
  • Give it about two seconds. Lifeboat saves when you pause typing, when you leave the field, and when you leave the page.
  • Was the site paused? Open Lifeboat and check whether the button in the top right says "Resume".
  • Was the tab already open when you installed? Freshly installed extensions can't reach tabs opened beforehand. Lifeboat tries to inject itself, but a reload settles it.
  • Password fields and recognised sensitive fields are excluded. These exclusions cannot be switched off. Sensitive text pasted into an ordinary comment box can still be saved; pause the site if you do not want it kept.
  • Install before you need a rescue. Lifeboat cannot recover text lost before installation. Chrome's protected pages and unsupported editors cannot be saved.

The rescue card didn't appear

It only appears when Lifeboat is confident something was genuinely lost: the text was saved, it is under a day old, you weren't the one deleting it, and you hadn't dismissed the card for that field in the last 24 hours. Sending a message in a chat box is understood as sending, not losing, so no card appears.

Still on 1.0.1? That version also required 25 words before showing the card. Version 1.0.2 removes that extra limit, so shorter saved drafts qualify too.

Your text is still saved either way — open Lifeboat from the toolbar and press Restore there.

Restore put my text on the clipboard instead

That happens when the original field no longer exists on the page — the site changed, or you're on a different page now. Paste it wherever you need it.

I bought Pro and the boats are still locked

  • Paste the key into the drawer (the ⚙ icon), then press Activate.
  • Keys work on up to 5 browsers. If you've hit that, email support and I'll reset it.
  • Lost your key? Email support from the address you bought with.

How do I delete everything Lifeboat saved?

In 1.0.2, choose Forget draft on a card to remove that draft and its saved versions, or open earlier versions and forget one. To clear the current site, open settings and choose Clear saved text on this site, then confirm. Undo is available for 15 seconds while the popup and Chrome stay open.

Deleting saved text does not pause new saving or remove Pro. Press Pause to stop saving new writing on a site. To remove all sites' data, uninstall the extension.

Is my typing sent anywhere?

No. Details in the privacy policy — short version: your text never leaves your computer. Pro sends licence details to Polar when activated and periodically to validate the licence, never your saved writing or page addresses.

Why did Lifeboat ask for a review?

After a real rescue, 1.0.2 shows an optional invitation inside the popup. It does not open the store automatically. Choose No thanks to dismiss it permanently. The test sample never triggers it.
