Lifeboat

Privacy Policy

The short version: Lifeboat saves the text you type into web forms so it can give it back when a page loses it. That text stays on your computer, encrypted. It is never sent anywhere by the extension. Free saving and recovery work offline. Pro sends licence details to Polar for activation and periodic validation; it never sends your saved text. There is no Lifeboat account to create.

What Lifeboat stores

  • Text you type into form fields, text areas and editors on websites you visit
  • The address of the page and a label for the field, so your text can be offered back in the right place
  • Your settings: which sites are paused, your chosen theme, and your saved-text preferences
  • Your licence key and activation identifier, if you buy Lifeboat Pro
  • Local save/rescue counts and whether you dismissed the optional review invitation. These are never sent as analytics.

All of it lives in your browser's local storage on your own device. Saved text is encrypted at rest using AES-GCM.

Which fields Lifeboat excludes

  • Passwords. Password fields are excluded in code, always.
  • Recognised payment fields. Card-number and security-code fields identified by their type, autocomplete hints or labels are excluded.
  • Recognised national-ID fields, including SSN and kennitala labels.

These field exclusions cannot be turned off. They do not inspect or classify every piece of writing: sensitive information pasted into an ordinary text box can still be saved. Pause saving on sensitive sites. Version 1.0.2 also adds deletion from the popup.

What leaves your device

Your saved text: never. Lifeboat has no server and no analytics.

If you enter a Pro licence key, Lifeboat contacts Polar, our payment provider, to activate and validate it. It checks again periodically (normally after a week; failed checks may retry). Requests include the licence key, organisation and activation identifiers, and a generic browser label at activation. They never contain your saved text or saved page addresses. Polar also receives the usual network connection details. Polar acts as merchant of record for the purchase and handles payment and tax; see their privacy policy.

How long it is kept

By default Lifeboat keeps the last 3 versions of each field for 7 days, then deletes them on its next scheduled cleanup (normally every six hours while Chrome runs). Saving starts at 25 characters by default. Lifeboat Pro lets you change retention to anywhere from 3 to 30 days and 1 to 10 versions. Deleted means deleted — there is no copy elsewhere.

Your control

  • See everything saved for a site in the extension popup
  • Pause Lifeboat on any site with one tap; it then saves nothing there
  • Export the latest saved text for each field on a site as a JSON file
  • In version 1.0.2, forget a draft and its versions, delete an earlier version, or clear a site without uninstalling. A 15-second Undo keeps only encrypted rows temporarily in worker memory; it ends sooner if Chrome stops that worker. It does not affect your Pro licence.
  • Remove the extension to delete everything it stored — nothing is left behind, because nothing was ever anywhere else

Children

Lifeboat is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
